01 — what is being measured?

Potential goes in. Current comes out.

Cyclic voltammetry sweeps the working-electrode potential forward and back while measuring current. The resulting current–potential curve is the CV. Its shape and how its peak current changes with scan rate can help distinguish transport and surface-response hypotheses.

    03 — the synthetic decision task

    One virtual sample; choose the next scan rate

    SYNTHETIC BENCHMARK

    The benchmark does not reuse the published curve above. It creates one unnamed virtual sample with a hidden current-scaling mechanism. Each policy may request scan rates from 5–500 mV/s. It sees noisy normalized responses, updates three probabilities, and stops when sufficiently confident or out of budget.

    Diffusionresponse ∝ √scan rate
    Surfaceresponse ∝ scan rate
    Mixedresponse = both contributions
    1. Policy selects scan rate→ 2. Validator checks 5–500 mV/s→ 3. Virtual instrument measures→ 4. Beliefs update→ 5. Continue or stop

    No physical sample is changed inside this synthetic campaign: all scan rates probe the same virtual sample. Sample replacement belongs to the real multi-formulation workflow above.
